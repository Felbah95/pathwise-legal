What PathWise is

PathWise helps students discover opportunities, understand how their profile compares with stated requirements, prepare documents and track applications. PathWise is not the provider of any opportunity and does not apply on your behalf. "Start Application" opens the provider's official page, and you apply there.

Matches are not guarantees

A match percentage is a comparison between your profile and the requirements we hold. It is not a guarantee that you are eligible or that you will be accepted. Always confirm deadlines, requirements and funding with the official provider before you rely on them.

Accuracy of opportunity information

We aim to list only information checked against an official source, and we mark which opportunities have been verified. Information can still change or be wrong. If you spot a problem, use "Report a problem" in the app.

AI explanations

AI explanations only restate information PathWise already holds. They can be incomplete or wrong, are not advice, and never replace the official provider's requirements.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep your login details private and give accurate information in your profile.
  • Only upload documents you have the right to use.
  • Do not misuse the service or try to access other people's data.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app or by following our account deletion page. We may suspend accounts that misuse the service.

PathWise Plus

PathWise Plus is an optional subscription. When you buy it through the App Store or Google Play, it renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you manage or cancel it in your store account settings. Where card payment is offered outside the app stores, you can cancel in the app. The price is shown before you pay. The free version keeps working without a subscription.
