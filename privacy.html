Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PathWise helps students find opportunities such as scholarships, internships and programmes, understand how their profile compares with the stated requirements, prepare documents and track applications. This policy explains what information the PathWise app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you have.

Who is responsible

PathWise is operated by Prince Felbah. Contact for privacy questions and requests: pfelbah95@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (the password is handled securely by our authentication provider and is never visible to us). If you choose Google sign-in, Google tells us your name and email address.
  • Profile information you enter: name, nationality, country, education (level, field, university, course, graduation year), skills, interests, goals, work experience, languages and preferences. You choose what to fill in; most of it is optional.
  • Your activity in the app: opportunities you save, your applications and notes, notification settings, and messages you send to support or reports you file.
  • Files you upload: documents you add to your private vault, and an optional profile photo.
  • Subscription status: whether you have PathWise Plus and until when. We never see your card number or payment details.
  • Language choice and a remembered email address on your device if you tick "Remember me" (stored only on your phone).

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera or microphone data, advertising identifiers, or device usage analytics. PathWise has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Why we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To compare your profile with opportunity requirements and explain the result, show what is missing, track your applications and send the reminders you asked for.
  • To provide optional features you choose, such as AI explanations and PathWise Plus.
  • To answer your support requests, review reports about opportunities, and keep the service safe.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use service providers who process information only to provide their service to PathWise:

  • Supabase – hosting, database, sign-in and private file storage. Your data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).
  • RevenueCat, the Apple App Store and Google Play – to process PathWise Plus purchases and tell us whether a subscription is active.
  • Stripe or Paystack – only where card payment is offered outside the app stores, to process that payment.
  • OpenAI – only if you use the optional AI help (PathWise Plus). When you tap an AI button on an opportunity, that opportunity's details and a few profile facts (study level, field, nationality, country, graduation year, skills, languages and interests) are sent to produce the explanation. Your name, email, documents and notes are never sent.
  • Google – only if you choose Google sign-in.

Other students cannot see your profile, applications, documents or notifications. A small number of support administrators can look up basic account details (name, email, country, study level) to help you; each lookup is recorded in an audit log. PathWise never sends your documents to any opportunity provider: you submit them yourself on the provider's official site.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How we protect it

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Access rules in our database mean each account can read only its own data. Documents are kept in private storage and links to open them expire after a couple of minutes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, profile, applications, saved items, notifications and uploaded files are permanently removed from our systems. Copies in the hosting provider's routine backups are removed as those backups expire. Records of support requests may be kept where needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal duty.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile, and change notification settings, in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to stop using it, by emailing pfelbah95@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Device notifications are optional and need your permission; you can turn them off in the app or your phone settings.

Children

PathWise is intended for students aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: pfelbah95@gmail.com.
