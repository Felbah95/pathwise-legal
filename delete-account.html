Delete your PathWise account

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open PathWise and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm.
This permanently deletes your account straight away.

Option 2: by email (if you cannot open the app)

Send an email from the address you registered with to pfelbah95@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PathWise account". We will confirm and delete your account, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile, goals, skills and preferences
  • Your saved opportunities, applications and notes
  • Your notifications and settings
  • Documents and photos you uploaded

Copies in the hosting provider's routine backups are removed as those backups expire. Support messages may be kept where needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal duty. If you have a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play, deleting your account does not cancel it: cancel it in your store account settings.
