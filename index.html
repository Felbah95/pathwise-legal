Honest by design

Clear about what PathWise is, and what it isn't

Students deserve straight answers. These are the rules PathWise is built on.

  • No promises about eligibilityA match is a comparison with the stated requirements, never a guarantee. Always confirm with the official provider.
  • PathWise never applies for youYou apply yourself on the official site. Your documents are never sent anywhere without your action.
  • Verified means checkedAn opportunity is only marked verified after it has been checked against an official source. Demo records are clearly labelled.
  • Your data stays yoursNo ads and no tracking. Other students cannot see your information, and you can delete your account at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is PathWise free?

Yes. You can create an account, get matches, track applications and keep documents for free, and that never expires. PathWise Plus is an optional subscription that removes the limits.

How much does Plus cost?

$0.99 per month, or $9.99 per year. It starts with a free trial of 5 days, and you can cancel before it ends to pay nothing. The app store shows the price in your own currency before you pay.

Does PathWise apply for me?

No. PathWise never submits an application or sends your documents anywhere. The Start Application button opens the provider's official page, and you apply there yourself.

How accurate is a match?

A match compares your profile with the requirements an opportunity states, and explains each part. It is a guide, not a guarantee of eligibility, so always confirm the final requirements with the official provider.

Who can see my documents and profile?

Only you. Documents are stored privately, and other students cannot see your information. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete my account. You can also follow the steps on the account deletion page.
